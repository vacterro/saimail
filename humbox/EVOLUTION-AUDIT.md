## Registered real-use plan

The immutable registration is
`../.saipen/evidence/T-142-evolution-audit/registration.json`. It precedes the
T-142 recorded observations and defines a six-entry opportunistic study. The
current root actor, saipen-cli, is the observer on real SAIMAIL Work T-142. No
independent sender, receiver or successor has been assigned. Their enrollment
status is NOT_ENROLLED, not an invented future identity. An existing admitted
actor may participate when its actual authority and Work binding permit it;
this registration authorizes no message, provider call or delegation itself.

Stage A records metadata-only entry on up to six actual Work entries spanning
at least three Work/context boundaries. Keep current host path, current Work,
seat, scopes, command/contract provenance, completion/continuation, reading
references and feedback basis. Preserve EMPTY, DEGRADED and partial results;
do not seed a mailbox to manufacture an eligible opportunity. Repeated commands
in one context do not count as distinct actors or independent sessions.

Stage B begins only on an eligible real letter for authorized existing Work.
Before opening, record the receiver's question, scope, planned baseline and
success/failure criterion. The receiver records its first assessment without
prior actors' answers; then explicitly reviews, rechecks evidence and chooses
a decision. Preserve negative/deferred/stale decisions and changed reasons.
Do not manufacture RESOLVED from a replay or harness verdict. The sender records
the revision it independently selects from that feedback and why it matters.

Receiver effort is observable actions: header pages, envelopes opened, evidence
files inspected, tool commands, retries, corrections and time from first relevant
observation to explicit decision. Process latency and serialized bytes are
separate proxies. If a transcript or timing boundary is unavailable, record
UNKNOWN. Self-report may state easier/harder/unchanged and its reason; it cannot
stand in for measured actions or establish general agent comfort.

Stage C enrolls a fresh successor, with actual identity/context provenance and
different real Work in the relevant scope. It obtains reserve through entry
discovery, rechecks original and result evidence, decides whether the lesson
applies, executes that Work and verifies its own resulting artifact. Neither
predecessor chat nor a scripted expected answer supplies its decision. A changed
condition may correctly produce STALE rather than reuse.

The strongest allowed outcome is one traceable field improvement with recorded
receiver effort, an independently selected revision and successor execution.
Claim a cost reduction only against the preregistered comparable baseline; with
no comparable baseline report observation only. General utility, comprehension,
model improvement and universal comfort remain outside this sample's claim.
At six entries without an eligible letter, end the study as EMPTY_OBSERVATIONS.
Unavailable actors or absent successor execution remain NOT_RUN/UNPROVEN.
