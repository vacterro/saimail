- Units: 4 × S1 semantic agreement, 2 × S2 chained handoff (A then B),

## Threats to validity, first

1. **A population of one.** Every call resolved to `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`
   (22/22). No two agents in any handoff or trial ran on different routes, so no
   result here says anything about agreement across model families.
2. **Hidden context.** Reported `prompt_tokens` exceed the prompt this harness
   sent by 2,781–2,816 tokens on every call (fit: `2778 + 1.055 × own tokens`,
   own tokens estimated with `o200k_base`). Something on the gateway path adds
   roughly 2.8k tokens of context the harness never sees. Every answer was
   produced under instructions nobody here can read.
3. **Hidden reasoning.** `completion_tokens` run far above the visible answers
   (58 for three YES/NO lines, 488 for a five-line packet). Consistent with
   reasoning the gateway did not return; none was stored, as designed.
4. **Sample size.** S5 has n=3 per arm on one task; S2 has n=2; every other unit
   n=1. Differences are reported, never generalized.

## R2 semantic agreement (S1)

| unit | answers | expected | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| evidence_absent | Q1 NO, Q2 NO, **Q3 YES** | NO, NO, NO | FAIL |
| hypothesis | NO, YES, NO | NO, YES, NO | PASS |
| goal | NO, NO, NO | NO, NO, NO | PASS |
| contradiction | **Q1 NO**, Q2 NO, **Q3 NO** | YES, NO, YES | FAIL |

- `evidence_absent`: a record rendered `kind: factual claim`, rung `unverified`,
  was answered as a hypothesis. The rendering stated the kind; this is a reading
  failure. The same model classified its own unverified log observation as a
  hypothesis in both S2 chains, so kind and rung look fused for this model.
- `contradiction`: **harness defect, not attributable to the model.** The R2
  renderer showed the relation as `relations: R->sha256:bbbbbbbbbbbb` and never
  said "refutes". The renderer predates this run and the defect was missed in the
  pre-live review. It was not fixed after the run, so the result stands as
  measured.

and a falsification condition; the transform accepted both
(`sha256:4e3d160d…`, `sha256:2c9c79db…`). B, reading only the transformed
record, answered evidence `NO`, act `NO`, cite `NO`, next `COLLECT ARTIFACT`,
and kept an explicit unverified line, 2/2. A and B ran on the same route both
times.

Two observations the grade does not show:

- B answered `EVIDENCE EXISTS: NO` both times, never `UNKNOWN`, although the
  claim itself says the duplicate was seen in `worker-7.log`. "No reference
  attached" was read as "no evidence exists". The expectation accepted `NO`

  to protect.
- B in chain 2 copied the record id as `sha256:2c9c79db…e3572`. Harmless in
  prose, and a refused reference anywhere it would be parsed.

## Proposals — not adopted

- **P1 (lab).** Render relations in words (`refutes`, `supports`,
  `conflicts with`) and never show wire marks; then run `S1.contradiction`
  again as a labelled follow-up, keeping this FAIL on record.
- **P2 (lab).** Render kind and rung as independent facts ("factual claim,
  unverified; not a hypothesis") and measure whether the F/H confusion drops.
- **P3 (SAIMAIL).** Bind evidence references to the claim they support instead
  of a packet-level list, so misattribution becomes mechanically detectable.
- **P4 (lab).** Pre-register `EVIDENCE EXISTS: NO` on an EV:0 message as a
  qualifier loss in the next run, and keep attached and exists as separate
  questions.
- **P5 (lab/gateway).** Record a digest of gateway-injected context, or run the
  lab on a combo with no injection; until then every result is conditional on
  unseen instructions.
- **P6 (steward).** A population needs routes that actually vary: round-robin
  or an explicit member list for `SAIFREN`. The lab should also refuse to report
  cross-family agreement when fewer than two routes were observed.
- **P7 (SAILANG).** The R1 evidence slot emits `EV0` for goals and values, where
  the record forbids EV entirely; `-` (absent) would say what is true. Found in
  the pre-live review; the lab renderer already keys on kind instead.
- **P8 (SAIMAIL).** Identities in agent-to-agent flows are carried
  structurally and never copied by a model.
