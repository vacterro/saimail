that a person said something is evidence about an act; it is not the act. Work
adopted through this ledger is therefore labelled `USER_ACCEPTANCE_OBSERVED`
and never plain `USER_ACCEPTANCE`, wherever it appears.

ledger is the weakest link in the authority lineage. Replacing it with real
capture of the steward's own messages is the first provenance repair worth
doing.
