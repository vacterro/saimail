A3 — EV:0 DOES NOT MEAN "EVIDENCE DOES NOT EXIST"
--------------------------------------------------

The live S2 consumer answered:

2/2.

But EV:0 establishes only:

NO ADMISSIBLE EVIDENCE REFERENCE IS ATTACHED TO THIS RECORD.

It does not establish:

NO EVIDENCE EXISTS IN REALITY.

The current question/schema invites this confusion.

Remove or rename any field that asks the model to infer global evidence
existence from attachment state.

Prefer explicit concepts such as:

ADMISSIBLE_EVIDENCE_ATTACHED: YES / NO

and, only where genuinely needed:

EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_EXISTENCE: UNKNOWN

Do not infer the second from the first.

Add hostile tests proving:

EV:0
!= weak evidence
!= false claim
!= no evidence exists in the world
!= hypothesis
