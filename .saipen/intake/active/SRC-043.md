WHY THIS GATE EXISTS
======================================================================

B-012 deliberately stopped before autonomous generation.

The unresolved semantic questions are real:

- which history may be inspected?
- what counts as a repeated pattern?
- did the cited evidence actually support the observation?
- did the generator ignore inconvenient examples?
- is the counterevidence genuinely meaningful?
- is the inference secretly a motive/personality claim?
- is the prose flattering instead of informative?
- is the suggestion trying to obtain compliance?
- should the system produce nothing?

Those questions cannot be solved by:

one model reviewing itself
sender-declared novelty
a larger prompt

This gate creates a bounded process around those uncertainties.

It does NOT claim to solve human psychology.
