# Modules

One row per shipped module. "Guarantees" are the contract the module enforces;
"Refuses" are the named errors it raises instead of guessing. Contracts are
normative in [spec/DECISIONS.md](https://github.com/vacterro/saimail/blob/main/spec/DECISIONS.md).
